## Verdict

**Sections 1, 2, 8 and 9 are largely accurate. Most of what is wrong sits in section 7.**

- **Section 2.** The variable tables match the code row by row, apart from a few omissions (the policy's own red margin, the pledge pool, the P2S cap, the event-stage bands, template alphas) and one wrong scope (the per-unit trade cap does not bind redemptions).
- **Section 7.** Four examples describe a mechanism that the 2aafbad model does not have:
  - **7.4:** a rate shock cannot be entered through the yield parameters, because rate returns are surprises against the expected path;
  - **7.11:** a "spreads widen 250bp" override raises credit returns instead of marking credit down;
  - **7.2:** the owner's draw after an oil crash reaches about 7%, not 8–9%, and an oil crash without a macro crisis is not a stress node;
  - **7.1:** a drought does not lower θ.

  In addition, 7.3 and 7.5 overstate magnitudes. The crisis regime has a positive stock–bond correlation, which contradicts both 7.4 and the parameter's own rationale, so it is probably a code bug.
- **REFINANCE and LEVER.** The definitions are stated correctly in §1.1. But §4.11 explains rule R1 backwards, and DSGO §1.2 does the same. §2.2 also labels financing by instrument, while R1 labels it by use.
- **Readability.** IC members are sent to §7 and to the "meaning" columns of §2. Both rely on row codes and symbols (H9, θ, ℓ, p*, IM, P2S) that are explained only in sections the IC is told to skip.

## 8. Code observations outside the document (for the prototype owner)

These came up while checking the document. None is on the parallel fix list, so the owner should decide whether to add them.

1. **Crisis correlation sign.** `factors.corr_crisis[equity][yield] = −0.30` contradicts its own rationale ("yields fall with equities (flight to quality)"). As coded, bonds fall with equities inside crisis quarters (FN-02).
2. **Availability fractions.** They are applied as on/off for every facility except the revolver, in both `model.py` and `sim.apply_node` (FN-04).
3. **REDEEM is outside the per-unit trade cap** (`ub[R] = 1`) (FN-13).
4. **Revolver upsize under the rule recourse.** `sim.run_policy` raises the revolver capacity to signed + L^up only inside the playbook branch at t = 1. Under the rule recourse the upsize fee is paid at t0 but the capacity never rises. This is latent: O1's L^up is 0 in 2aafbad.
5. **Forced paydowns in the simulator.** Year-end forced paydowns cover the NAV facility (LTV) and the revolver (availability) only. CS and SMA back-leverage and the RA cash-out are not forced down outside playbook years.
6. **Level parameters carry no P&L.** Rate and credit returns are surprises against the expected path, so `factors.rt.start`, `factors.rt.mean`, `factors.cr.start` and `factors.cr.level` move levels and carry but create no loss. If this is the intended design, say so in the `params.json` rationales; otherwise a scenario-shock term is needed (FN-01, FN-10, FN-11).
7. **Hard-coded constants** (FN-31).
8. **The SEC_BUY "stage not Black" gate cannot bind**, because B0 keeps the 4% floor at every node, so stage Black never occurs.
9. **R1 at the root.** `r1_labels` uses the gross $10bn status-quo beta, not its net-of-margin $9.4bn, as the root's prior synthetic financing. This understates the root's ΔB by about $0.6bn; the effect is immaterial.
