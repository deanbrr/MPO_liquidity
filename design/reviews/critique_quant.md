# Critique (quant-consistency lens): ARCHITECTURE.md v1.0 and BUILD_SPEC.md v1.0

Reviewer: adversarial design critic, quant-consistency lens · Date: 2026-10-18 · Read in full: `design/ARCHITECTURE.md` (ARCH), `design/BUILD_SPEC.md` (SPEC). Checked against `design/00_DESIGN_BRIEF.md` (brief, incl. §7–§9 and R16), `research/00_SYNTHESIS.md` v1.1 (SYN) §0–§4, `research/addenda/gap_01`–`gap_05`, `research/groups/addenda/gap_01`–`gap_04` (G-gap_0k), the groups synthesis and the literature synthesis.

> **SYNTHETIC – NOT ADIC DATA.** Every balance, probability, rate and timing quoted below comes from the synthetic working book, the research prototypes or stylised venv calculations.

## Verdict

The two documents are faithful to the research on most points. λ is read only from the restored, feasible problem, and Phase I runs per consent scenario. Readiness-Red is split from event-Red. T6 is banded by model class, the overlay is a position layer with OV1–OV3, F_t is kept distinct from k_MS, and the owner draw is exogenous. SPEC's S-ADRs adopt every item of the brief's §7 literature sweep and the §8 interim Real Assets rules. The governance apparatus is unusually complete.

The quant core still has formulation errors that would produce wrong prices or wrong feasibility verdicts if built as written. Three are critical:
- **The Tier-1B floor is structurally infeasible as specified.** Its worst-model test already fails in M3 and M6 on the working book. SPEC also silently shortens the window to 5 years, after which it fails in every world.
- **The belief-to-engine interface double-counts beta.** BeliefSet carries a *total* forward return, but every engine adds it to its own beta return.
- **The red-event definition has a code-path loophole.** A "preemptive (planned)" rung inside the forced-recourse waterfall sells PE and RA interests without counting them as red events.

Nine more issues are high:
- five errors in H: unfunded commitments are not transferred on sale; terminal wealth ignores Real Assets debt and posted IM; the red margin double-counts liquidity it converts; financing and impact costs are charged twice;
- a T4′ calibration that re-imposes the M6 veto ADR-11 removed;
- a T4′ appetite row with two incompatible definitions, one of which the specified stress replica cannot compute;
- LLM-produced probabilities in the IC pack (P1, R16);
- the user-confirmed facts on the three return series (brief §9) are not adopted;
- a P performance budget whose component budgets sum to 95 s against a 60 s target.

**Recommendation.** Do not freeze contracts at the end of Wave 0 until QUANT-01 to QUANT-12 are closed by S-ADRs. QUANT-01, -02, -03 and -11 change frozen contracts: `FloorSpec`, `Tier1Spec`, `BeliefSet`/`draw_paths`, `SaleReason` semantics and `Mark`/synthetic observation shapes.
