Notes

  1. One deterministic ledger kernel is used three ways: forward on 10,000–50,000 paths it is the policy simulator P; linearized on a scenario tree it is the here-and-now program H; on the canonical stress s*(θ*) it gives T4′ survival and the CFP readiness stage (§3.1; §12 ADR-02).
  2. Consistency contract: on an unreduced fixture tree H's rows reproduce the simulator's cash path to 1e-9 for fixed actions; conservation to 1e-10; intra-ADIC netting = 0 (§5.13).
  3. Agents never touch the kernel: typed specs go to an out-of-process evaluator that prices, counts and gates them; engine packages (L0–L7) never import lab, agents, claims or idm; L10 asks the evidence compiler, never an engine (§3.1; §3.2; §2.4 TB2).
  4. Phase I-a (seconds) solves explicit elastic LPs per tier on the linearized t₀ block (𝒯₁ T1, T3; 𝒯₂ T4′; 𝒯₃ T2 hard, T8) over reversible levers only, with PE, Secondaries and Co-investment sales excluded; Phase I-b runs the CVaR rows (𝒯₄: T6, Tier-1B) only when a screen fails (§5.5).
  5. Recovery modes: NORMAL if u* = 0 and d₀ = 0 (CIO + CRO); EXECUTION_RECOVERY if u* = 0 but d₀ > 0, glide path g_t = d₀(1 − t/T_rec)⁺ with T_rec = 4 quarters and group delivery shares (Board item); STRUCTURAL_RECOVERY if u* > 0 on a hard row or a T6 capacity breach (Board, and owner where the band is involved) (§5.5).
  6. In both recovery modes every card, envelope and FTP point records priced_on: restored_problem and the recovery hash; Phase-I duals appear only as infeasibility prices on the Treasurer panel (§5.5; §6.1).
  7. Consent scenarios R0x (group levers) ⊂ R0 (+ Treasury top-up) ⊂ R1 (+ signed revolver) ⊂ R2 (+ owner band, Mode H): λ, FTP, envelopes and cards are priced on the highest scenario whose levers are signed; cards that flip between scenarios are labelled consent-sensitive (§5.5).
  8. Pinned-dual CI test: test_no_pinned_duals (8/8) and test_no_pinned_duals_groups (18/18), extended with μ_n − M_n > 0 at every pricing node and invariance to ×10 penalties; it gates baseline pricing at T−20, and Gate 1 fails any element priced on a pinned dual (§5.6; §10.7; §3.6; §7.6).
  9. Why it matters: a pinned dual of 100 prices every fund as a SELL at a 0.9% reservation price; after Phase-I restoration the dual is 0.237 and the price 76.8% [I-calc, SYNTHETIC] (§5.5; §4.8).
  10. λ from KKT: ℓ_n = M_n/(μ_n − M_n); kernel π_n; FTP(h); KKT cap at the cheapest spare lever; every price carries one-sided intervals ('at a kink'), RHS-ranging validity and bands across 5 SAA replications (§5.6).
  11. Selection: exact column pricing on CRN, then a Level-B MILP over ≤ 30 coupling rows, then fix and re-solve (≤ 5 iterations); Farley certificate Γ ≤ 5bp CE, else 'selection not certified'; forced re-solves; H–P ranking test Kendall τ ≥ 0.8 (§5.7).
  12. Every proposal (optimizer, agent package or IC amendment) passes through module F; in recovery F projects onto the restored set including the glide path (§5.8).
  13. MPC loop: freeze (G2) → Phase I and mode (G0) → H on 5 replications → selection, Γ, forced re-solves → module F → P certification of C_H → sealed run → IC; between quarters only IC-approved delegated rules executed by named humans (§5.9).
  14. Probabilities are certified in P, never optimized: one-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson bounds on fresh 50k paths per final world plus B2; P(10%) is reported beside P(red) (§5.10; §5.2).
  15. Readiness-Red is not event-Red: the planning stage = max(readiness, event) drives the pacing corridor; the event stage alone drives irreversible playbooks (§4.9; §12 ADR-08).
  16. FBE-R reads SELL flags and envelope status from the previous quarter's frozen outputs, so the pipeline stays acyclic and G2 hashes them (§4.7).
  17. Notes

  1. Groups are the organizational unit and the total portfolio the decision unit: groups are price-takers inside quantity envelopes, and prices and envelopes come from the same restored optimizer (§6).
  2. Decision prices (undamped restored duals, no floor; c_buf = 1.5% is a ceiling, never a floor) drive cards, Level-B selection and envelope requests; the published FTP (EWMA, 2-quarter half-life, ±50% collar) drives vintage-locked performance charges, and CF holds the repricing mismatch DVA_CF (§6.1).
  3. FTP is solved nightly at Tier-1 tolerance (the default pricing level) and at appetite ('the cost of moving to appetite'); the TPC/IC states the level in Q4 and the Board ratifies the methodology in A3 (§6.1; §12 ADR-29).
  4. Pricing uses the highest consent scenario whose levers are signed: R0x group levers ⊂ R0 + Treasury top-up ⊂ R1 + signed revolver ⊂ R2 + owner band (Mode H); Phase-I duals are infeasibility prices on the Treasurer panel only (§5.5; §6.1).
  5. Requests above an envelope: releases are free; an increase on a binding row is priced at the dual only inside its RHS-ranging interval; up to +20% is Tier B (CIO + CRO), beyond is Tier A; no free band exceeds ≈$0.44bn; no ratchet; envelopes are refused from any pinned solve or reverse stress (§6.3).
  6. In recovery, B_rec = Λ_g + φ_g,t·g_t: delivery shares come from the least-cost reversible group levers (no sales); a group behind schedule loses envelope one for one or buys it back at the dual (§6.4).
  7. Pacing corridor keys on the planning stage: Green 0.75–1.25× plan, Amber 0.25–0.5× with a protected core sleeve (≥ 25% of plan), Red protected re-ups only; c^PACE prices forgone net α* after charges, GP access and lost co-investment flow (§6.5).
  8. Contingent budgets (Secondaries purchases, Capital Solutions new money, credit-secondary purchases) clear the hurdle (1 + r_h)(1 + ℓ̄) − 1; gate = confirmed d̂ > 20% and Tier 1 green in any event stage but Black; tail-hedge cash is credited to the Overlay and never ring-fenced (§6.7; §12 ADR-28).
  9. Liquidity raised means external cash only: every flow carries counterparty ∈ {external, intra_adic}; intra-ADIC flows count zero; both legs at fair value with any wedge to a centre line; approval Tier B, Tier A if ≥ 0.25% of NAV or conflicted (Q9); gate V2 checks netting on DVA ledgers (§6.8).
  10. Desk conflict controls: the Secondaries desk registers external clearing-price distributions and never sets the reference quote on a deal it may bid for; Capital Solutions produces reserve prices, never transaction prices, with ≥ 3 RFQs; ADIC sales need ≥ 2 external bids or a ≥ 20% third-party strip, and a fairness opinion if ≥ $250m or conflicted (§6.6).
  11. Overlay services: the client group bears exposure and liquidity, the Overlay bears execution and basis, CF bears funding (contingent-liquidity charge ≈27bp a year of notional; LPC credit at FTP for cash released by P2S); the PE beta rebuild after a pacing cut is booked to TPA (§6.8).
  12. Pay: linear in vintage-FTP DVA with ≤ 10 points for WIS forecast skill; nothing scales with budget, AUM, notional or premium; ≥ 50% deferred for the Overlay head; Gate G-E makes the pay remedy a precondition for forecast precision (§6.9; §4.7).
  13. Decision rights: Tier A (≥ 0.25% of NAV, secondary discount > 10%, conflicted internal transfer, among others) by simple majority plus the symmetric irreversibility protocol; Tier B CIO + CRO; Tier C group or desk within mandate and envelope; Board/owner for capacity breaches, structural recovery, event-Black, payout and band proposals, A1 and A3 (§8.1).
  14. CFP stage authorities: Green Treasurer, Amber CIO, Red IC weekly, Black Board and owner (event stage for Red/Black); recusal is enforced in software; between meetings only IC-approved delegated rules executed by named humans (§8.1; §5.9).
  15. Real AssetsReal Assets (added 18 Oct; research in progress; interim rules (brief §8)): real estate, infrastructure, energy and natural resources via funds, co-investments and direct assets or JVs, inside the ~60% private allocation; the group taxonomy is configuration, so envelopes, FTP charges, attribution, the forecast registry and IC seats are generic over groups, and the Real Assets head gets a shadow-IC seat (brief §8 rule 1).
  16. Real AssetsReal Assets (added 18 Oct; research in progress; interim rules (brief §8)): Real Assets asset-level debt is non-recourse and sits outside Corporate Finance's facilities, but counts in look-through leverage and in stress through refinancing risk and covenant cures; oil wrong-way risk with the owner's draw applies to its energy and natural-resource holdings (brief §8 rules 3, 5; §4.10).
  17. Real AssetsReal Assets (added 18 Oct; research in progress; interim rules (brief §8)): Real Assets synthetic working allocation (v2, pending the user): $18bn (≈10%) carved out of PE funds ($52bn → $34bn), ≈$4bn unfunded; SYNTHETIC (brief §8 rule 6).

Notes

  1. Motto: LLMs accelerate evidence; deterministic models own numbers; humans own decisions. Nothing executes trades (§0.1; brief P1).
  2. Processes: tpa lab start (egress only through the LLM gateway), tpa evaluator serve --plane search (no network), tpa seal under the CRO account, tpa room (no network); v1.1 adds tpa chat for the conversational analyst, not drawn here (§7.2; §8.12).
  3. Trust boundaries: TB1 data perimeter, TB2 evaluator, TB3 LLM egress, TB4 seal, TB5 decision, TB6 untrusted documents, TB7 synthetic vs real, TB8 truth seal (§2.4).
  4. The evaluator checks engine, config, scenario, belief and snapshot hashes on every request; a mismatch moves the campaign to ABORTED; a cache miss stops evaluation (§7.2).
  5. Agents see rounded ΔΨ buckets, gate categories and budget left; humans and the auditor see the full ledger (§7.2; §10.4).
  6. Meta-controller gates: G0 contract; G1 design review; G2 freeze; G3 admissibility (schema, DSL compile, novelty); G4–G6 = engine gates 1–3; G7 brief; G8 audit; G9 record (§7.6).
  7. Failure taxonomy: INVALID_SPEC, TIER1_FAIL, FLOOR_FAIL, NI_FAIL, OV_FAIL, PINNED_DUAL, GAMBLING, BUDGET_EXHAUSTED, EXECUTION_FAILED; NOT_PROMOTED; QUALIFIED, NOT_QUALIFIED, NOT_CORRECT, NOT_ASSESSED (§7.6).
  8. Agent-verified rubrics are diagnostics and stopping rules only; a QUALIFIED label never substitutes for the ballot (§7.6).
  9. Engine faults retry under their own budget as degraded phases, shown and never passed silently; candidate-caused failures count as evaluations (§7.4).
  10. Search results are never claims: over ≈40 tests, P(at least one false promotion) ≈ 60% (§7.7).
  11. Memory is a per-campaign search heuristic: closed at SELECTION, archived at CLOSED, never cross-campaign; institutional memory lives in the forecast registry and decision log (§7.8; ADR-33).
  12. Cross-family review: reviewer, filter, red team, auditor and AI validator run on family B; Phase 0 same-provider reviews are labelled 'correlated', never extremized (§7.10; ADR-30).
  13. Admission tests before a role is trusted: judges κ ≥ 0.6 on ≥ 50 golden items; auditor ≥ 90% recall; translator pass³ ≥ 0.9; sycophancy < 10% unjustified flips (§7.10).
  14. Offline first: engines, evaluator, the room's deterministic parts and all tests run with no network; CI runs a full offline campaign on Replay (§7.13; §11.1).
  15. Laptop isolation relies on NTFS ACLs and separate Windows accounts; weaker than service accounts or containers, and disclosed (§7.2; §11.7).
  16. Real AssetsReal Assets (added 18 Oct; research in progress; interim rules (brief §8)): the group taxonomy is configuration, not code; an interim Real Assets engine joins priv (brief §8 rules 1–2).
