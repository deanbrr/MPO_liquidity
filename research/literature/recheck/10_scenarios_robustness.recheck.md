**Method**
- **Metadata** (authors, year, title, venue, volume/issue/pages, DOI) was checked against the Crossref REST API (`api.crossref.org/works/<DOI>` and `works?query.bibliographic=`), OpenAlex single-DOI lookups, and arXiv abstract pages.
- **Usage**, meaning a finding the dossier attributes to a work, was checked against the abstract. Sources: the dossier's own IDEAS/RePEc links (22 opened, all resolved to the cited paper), the OpenAlex abstract index and arXiv.
  - Two WebSearch calls covered usage points the abstracts could not settle (Carr–Wu, Genest–Zidek).
  - Consensus was not used.
- **Rate limits**
  - OpenAlex search returned HTTP 429 (Retry-After ≈9.8 h) after one call. OpenAlex single-DOI lookups kept working.
  - Crossref throttled parallel list queries, so they were run one at a time.
  - No verdict depends on a blocked call.
