## 1. Headline

- **Goal.** 10% a year time-weighted, gross of distributions, on economic NAV over 5 years: W0 = $169.8bn economic (177 reported), W* = $273.4bn.
- **The main objective (GBO) is PLAN.** With every lens available, E*[W~] = $285.2bn exceeds W*, so GBO minimizes bPOE: Phi* = 0.957 (status quo bPOE = 1.000).
- **In the tree** (210 leaves): P(goal) 46.7% for O1 against 43.2% for the status quo; CVaR10 of TWR -6.2% against -7.1% (the depth guard holds at delta = 0).
- **Out of sample** (10,000 fresh paths, sealed truth that the beliefs did not generate): P(goal) **46.9%** for O1 (O1 + rule recourse, chosen on the selection set) against 42.9% for the status quo and 44.7% for the best simple rule (B3); paired difference vs status quo +4.0pp (95% CI +3.6pp to +4.4pp); mean TWR 9.12% vs 8.60%.
- **In dollars:** expected 5-year distribution-inclusive wealth is $9.9bn above the status quo and $2.8bn below the best simple rule (B3; out of sample). Mean terminal wealth rewards dispersion, so a rule that levers to 1.30x can show more of it while reaching the goal less often and with a deeper tail. For comparison, the 3-book tree demo's optimizer beat its simple rule by about $0.04bn of certainty equivalent.
- **Value at equal tail** (vertical distance above the risk-matched beta line at O1's own CVaR10): **+2.9pp** (95% bootstrap CI +2.5pp to +3.2pp). This is the part of the uplift that plain synthetic beta cannot buy.
- **Pre-registered tests (Holm-adjusted):** H1 ESTABLISHED, H2 ESTABLISHED, H3 NOT ESTABLISHED, H6 ESTABLISHED. H4 (no gambling signature, SSD vs status quo): fail. H5 (MILP overfits more than GBO): pass. H7 (placebo): fail.
- **Recourse.** The pre-committed nearest-node playbook and a rule-based recourse (need-based funding, forced deleveraging, pacing and overlay held) were both certified; the selection set chose **rule** for O1. The t0 change list is the same in both; only what happens after year 0 differs (section 4).

## 7. Fused beliefs per holding (raw marks vs fused belief)

Raw marks are since-inception (sunk) and are shown for reference; every signal is restated as an annualized forward log return on nowcast economic NAV, debiased by Buhlmann credibility on the quarterly archive, and fused with precision weights shrunk halfway to equal weights where the archive is thin. The decision belief excludes the holding's own bid (leave-one-out). ## 10. What the results say

- **Where the value comes from.** Removing a family costs the most P(goal) for: PACE (-11.9pp OOS, -8.8pp at equal tail; needed a Phase I allowance), HEDGE (-2.8pp OOS, -2.4pp at equal tail), REBALANCE (-2.2pp OOS, -0.9pp at equal tail), REDEEM (-0.8pp OOS, -0.6pp at equal tail). Families whose removal *helps* (positive dP) are ones the LP over-uses in the tree: ADD +0.3pp.
- **Pacing is load-bearing for the hard liquidity rows.** Holding every commitment budget at plan makes the H9 red-event limit infeasible (Phase I allowance u* = 0.0027); the status quo itself breaches it on a few paths (it is not subject to the row). O1 cuts the commitment budgets with the lowest post-horizon value per $ (secondaries, real assets, private credit, Capital Solutions), which lowers stressed calls, and keeps PE above plan. The -11.9pp of the PACE row therefore mixes the value of the pacing mix with the cost of meeting the H9 red-event limit some other way at plan pacing.
- **Beta is risk budget, not value.** Fixing synthetic beta at the status quo changes P(goal) by -0.4pp but the tail by +0.87pp of CVaR10; at equal tail the change is +0.3pp. The risk-matched line shows what plain beta buys: from 1.00x to 1.10x, +1.6pp of P(goal) for -2.04pp of CVaR10.
- **The three-mark fusion adds +0.3pp of P(goal) in the headline book** (replacing fused beliefs with group priors: -0.3pp). On the null-information placebo book it adds +0.1pp, as it should. But O1 still sits +2.1pp above the risk-matched line on the placebo book, so most of O1's value does not come from belief-versus-bid selection: it comes from levers that do not use the marks (the opportunistic-book size, hedge-fund redemptions recycled into lower-cost beta, the pacing mix and the liquidity plan). H7 was pre-registered on the premise that value is belief-driven; it fails, and that is the finding.
- **The max-P(goal) comparison (O2).** HiGHS branch and bound on the exact MILP did not leave the root node within the budget (DSGO expects 30-90 minutes at full scale), so O2 is the MILP relaxation's bound plus a deterministic LP-rounding incumbent: in-tree P(goal) 48.0% against GBO's 46.7%, with the relaxation bound at 86.0% (gap 79%). Even this partial maximizer overfits the tree: its out-of-sample P(goal) is below O1's (H5), which is the reason the spec makes it a comparison and never the recommendation.
- **The t0 decision carries the value; the pre-committed year-1 playbook does not add to it out of sample:** O1 with the playbook 44.4% vs rule-based recourse 46.9%. Year-1 contingent plans are fitted to the 21 leaf paths below each stage-1 node; they are best read as contingency guidance to re-optimize at the year-1 review, not as a pre-commitment.
- **The red-event limit does not bind** across the frontier (P(goal) OOS varies by +0.0pp from the tightest to the unbounded limit; P(red) OOS at most 0.09%): with a $56bn public book, $15bn of Treasury and a $10bn revolver, the stressed 12-month red margin is negative on every tree path. The binding guards are the multi-level depth guard (delta) and the class / cushion leverage caps (A20 and the delta frontier).
- **Tail.** The depth guard holds in the tree, but out of sample O1's CVaR10 is -8.25% against -6.79% for the status quo, so the SSD certificate fails (H4) and the in-tree tail protection does not fully carry to fresh paths. The value-at-equal-tail measure already charges for this.

**Error ledger.**

- **E1** (annual LP ledger vs quarterly simulator on the tree's own 210 paths): B0 LP 43.2% vs simulator on the same paths 44.9%; O1 rule LP 46.7% vs simulator 45.2%; O1 playbook LP 46.7% vs simulator 44.8%. - **E5** (belief risk: one driver in the tree vs the simulator's full truth and idiosyncratic noise): O1 tree 46.7% vs fresh paths with truth = belief 42.7% and with the sealed truth 46.9%.
